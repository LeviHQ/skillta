# Standardize page breadcrumbs

## Scope
- Add a visible breadcrumb to every routed content page that does not currently have one.
- Move every existing breadcrumb to the top-right of its page content.
- Keep multi-level paths such as `Home / Blog / Category` and `Home / Roadmaps / Career` intact.
- Use wrapping and right alignment so long paths remain readable on mobile.
- Leave the homepage and 404 screen without breadcrumbs.

## Pages
- Standardize: About, Blog, Contact, Quiz, Results, Roadmap Library, Roadmap Detail, Compare Careers, Salary Predictor, Resume Reviewer, Resume Version Control, Skill Gap Analyzer, and Blog Post.
- Add missing: Dashboard, Story, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and country guide pages.

## Verification
- Check the app health after edits.
- Verify representative desktop and mobile pages visually.
- Provide the complete page list for manual review.
