# About SkillTa

We believe everyone deserves clarity about their career path. SkillTa uses AI to match your unique traits with the perfect tech career.

## Our Mission

Help aspiring tech professionals find their ideal career path with honest, data-driven guidance — not hype.

## How It Works

Our AI analyzes your interests, skills, and personality across 10 carefully crafted questions to find your best match.

## Why We Built This

Too many people waste time on the wrong career path. We want to save you months of confusion with clear, actionable roadmaps.

## What's Next
