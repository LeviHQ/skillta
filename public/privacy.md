# Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: October 24, 2026

At SkillTa (accessible via **skillta.tech**), we respect your privacy. We never sell your personal data. Your uploaded resumes and quiz responses are stored securely and privately in encrypted storage. We never use your uploaded resumes to train public foundational AI models. Active Pro and Lifetime users enjoy a 100% ad-free experience.

## 1. Introduction & Overview

SkillTa ("we", "our", or "us"), operated under skillta.tech, is dedicated to helping individuals discover, plan, and advance their tech careers through AI-driven diagnostic assessments, roadmap generation, ATS resume optimization, and Resume Version Control.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how it is processed and protected, and what rights you have regarding your data under applicable global privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA principles). By using SkillTa, you consent to the data practices described herein.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information only to provide and improve our educational and career guidance tools:

- **Authentication & Account Details:** When you sign in via Google OAuth (managed securely through Firebase), we collect your verified name, email address, and profile picture URL. We never receive or store your Google account password.

- **Assessment & Diagnostic Data:** Your answers to the SkillTa AI Career Quiz, selected work preferences, target roles, quiz scores, and earned achievement badges are stored to deliver continuous personalized recommendations.

- **Resume & Document Data:** When you utilize the AI Resume Reviewer or Resume Version Control, you may upload documents in PDF or TXT format. We extract text content strictly to compute ATS compatibility scores, identify keyword gaps, and deliver improvement tips. Uploaded files are stored in private cloud buckets bound strictly to your user ID.

- **Technical & Device Telemetry:** Standard non-identifying telemetry including browser user-agent, operating system, language preference, IP address, and interaction metrics to protect our service against abuse.

## 3. How We Process and Use Your Data

We process your data strictly for legitimate educational, operational, and delivery purposes:

- To generate personalized career roadmaps and real-time ATS resume diagnostic scores.

- To maintain your historical versions in Resume Version Control so you can track ATS improvements over time.

- To manage your Pro or Lifetime subscription entitlements and deliver plan receipts via email.

- To maintain platform security, prevent automated web-scraping, and resolve technical bugs.

## 4. Third-Party Sub-processors & Service Providers

To provide a secure, scalable SaaS experience, we partner with industry-leading infrastructure providers. All service providers are vetted for strict confidentiality and security compliance:

- **Firebase / Google Cloud:** Secure single-sign-on (SSO) authentication and user session verification.

- **Backend & Database Infrastructure:** Enterprise-grade database and private storage with Row Level Security (RLS) ensuring strict tenant isolation.

- **Dodo Payments:** Our authorized Merchant of Record and payment gateway. When you purchase SkillTa Pro ($3/year) or Lifetime ($10), your payment details (credit cards, UPI, PayPal) are processed directly on Dodo Payments' PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant infrastructure. SkillTa never sees, handles, or stores your financial credit card numbers.

- **Resend:** Secure transactional email gateway used exclusively to deliver your account confirmation, payment receipts, and limit notifications.

- **Adsterra:** Independent advertising network that serves contextual banners strictly to non-paying guests.

## 5. Advertising, Cookies & Paid Ad-Free Experience

SkillTa uses cookies and browser local storage solely to retain your user session, maintain UI theme preferences (Light/Dark mode), and track quiz state during an active session.

**Ad-Free Guarantee:** Active subscribers of SkillTa Pro and SkillTa Lifetime receive an unconditional 100% ad-free experience. Ad scripts are dynamically prevented from executing whenever an active paid entitlement is detected.

For free guest visitors, contextual ads may be served via Adsterra. These providers may place standard third-party advertising cookies or analyze device headers under their respective privacy policies. You can disable third-party cookies at any time via your browser settings. We never share your account details or uploaded documents with advertisers.

## 6. Data Security, Storage & Retention

We employ comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards:

- **Encryption:** All web traffic is strictly encrypted using HTTPS/TLS 1.3 in transit, and stored assets are encrypted at rest.

- **Access Control:** Database tables use strict Row-Level Security policies so that only your authenticated Google account can read or write your history.

- **Self-Service Deletion:** In Resume Version Control, clicking "Delete" on any version permanently and immediately purges that file from our private storage buckets.

- **Retention Policy:** We retain account data as long as your account remains active. Inactive data is periodically audited and can be permanently removed upon request.

## 7. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Regardless of your geographic location, SkillTa grants you full transparency and authority over your information:

- **Right to Access:** You can view all saved quiz results, badges, and resume versions directly within your Dashboard.

- **Right to Portability & Download:** You can download your uploaded resume versions and career roadmaps at any time.

- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You may request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated records by contacting us.

- **Right to Rectification:** You can modify or upload updated resume versions whenever desired.

## 8. Children's Privacy

SkillTa is designed for students, university graduates, and professionals exploring technical careers. Our services are not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from minors. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal data, we will promptly delete it.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our platform or updates to statutory requirements. Whenever material modifications are made, we will update the "Last Updated" timestamp at the top of this document. Continued use of SkillTa following any changes signifies your informed acceptance.
