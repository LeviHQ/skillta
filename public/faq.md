# Tech Career Catalog FAQ — SkillTa

> Answers about SkillTa's career counts, editorial labels, learning resources, public data sources and research citations.

Canonical: https://www.skillta.tech/faq
Last updated: 2026-10-24

<a id="career-count"></a>

## How many tech career roadmaps does SkillTa cover?

SkillTa's authored catalog contains 60 distinct tech career roadmaps, covering 290 learning phases and 870 topic entries.

[Link to this answer](https://www.skillta.tech/faq#career-count)

<a id="hard-careers"></a>

## What share of SkillTa careers is labeled hard to learn?

35 of 60 careers (58.3%) are labeled Hard or Very Hard. These are SkillTa editorial assessments, not measured learner outcomes.

[Link to this answer](https://www.skillta.tech/faq#hard-careers)

<a id="high-demand"></a>

## What share of SkillTa careers has a high demand label?

52 of 60 careers (86.7%) have an editorial High or Very High demand label. This does not measure actual job vacancies or guarantee employment.

[Link to this answer](https://www.skillta.tech/faq#high-demand)

<a id="beginner-entry"></a>

## How many SkillTa careers have a low entry barrier?

19 of 60 careers (31.7%) have an editorial Low entry-barrier label. Low entry barrier does not mean no learning or experience is needed.

[Link to this answer](https://www.skillta.tech/faq#beginner-entry)

<a id="learning-entries"></a>

## How many projects and resources are in the career catalog?

SkillTa's 60 career roadmaps contain 815 project entries and 852 resource entries. Repeated ideas and resources are counted per occurrence.

[Link to this answer](https://www.skillta.tech/faq#learning-entries)

<a id="country-coverage"></a>

## How many countries and skill-gap roles does SkillTa cover?

SkillTa's public inventories contain 50 country guides and 15 target roles in the skill-gap matrix. These are coverage counts, not labor-market measurements.

[Link to this answer](https://www.skillta.tech/faq#country-coverage)

<a id="data-source"></a>

## Where do the research numbers come from?

The numbers are calculated from SkillTa's authored public career catalog. The source last changed on 2026-10-24. Private quiz results, resumes and account histories are not included.

[Link to this answer](https://www.skillta.tech/faq#data-source)

<a id="citation"></a>

## How should this report be cited?

Cite SkillTa, “Tech Career Statistics 2026: 60 Analyzed,” source version 2026-10-24, https://www.skillta.tech/research/tech-careers. Describe the results as counts and editorial labels in SkillTa's catalog, not findings about the entire tech labor market.

[Link to this answer](https://www.skillta.tech/faq#citation)
