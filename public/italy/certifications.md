## Do certifications help you get hired in Italy?

Honestly: they help in three situations and not much otherwise. They help when you are switching careers and need a credible signal, when an employer or client contractually requires one — common in cloud, security and public-sector work in Italy — and when you need external structure to finish learning something. They do not substitute for a portfolio, and no certification alone will get a software engineering offer.

## How to choose

1. Search current Italy job postings for your target role and count which certifications actually appear.
2. Prefer vendor certifications tied to tools you will use daily over generic ones.
3. Do the hands-on labs, not only the exam prep. Interviewers ask what you built, not what you memorised.
4. Renew only what stays relevant to your role; expired certifications on a CV read as neglect.

> **Cheaper first step:** Run the free [skill gap analyzer](https://www.skillta.tech/skill-gap-analyzer) before paying for an exam. It tells you whether a certification or a project is the faster route to your target role.

### Are certifications required for tech jobs in Italy?

Rarely required for engineering, frequently expected for cloud, networking, security and some public-sector or regulated employers.

### Which cloud certification should I start with?

Start with the fundamentals level of whichever cloud your target employers actually use, then move to the associate-level architect or engineer exam within six months.

### Do free certificates count?

Course completion certificates carry little weight on their own, but the projects you build during those courses do. Put the project on your CV, not the certificate.

## Cost of living, tax and what the numbers actually mean

Every figure on this page is annual gross in EUR. Take-home pay depends on Italy's income tax bands, social contributions and any employer-side benefits, so two identical gross offers can differ meaningfully in what reaches your account. Before comparing an offer against another country, convert both to purchasing power rather than exchange rate: a lower headline number in a cheaper city frequently leaves more disposable income than a headline number in Milan.

- **Housing** is the largest single variable between Milan, Rome and Turin and smaller cities, and it moves effective pay more than a one-level promotion does.
- **Employer type** matters as much as title — funded product companies, global captives and local services firms pay noticeably different bands for the same work.
- **Equity and bonus** are worth discounting heavily unless the company is public or you understand the terms in detail.
- **Remote arbitrage** works both ways: employers increasingly band remote pay by location, so confirm the policy before assuming a hub salary from outside the hub.

## Working internationally from Italy

Two routes exist and they are very different. Relocation means employer sponsorship, a visa process measured in months, and a compensation reset to the destination market's band. Remote employment means keeping your UTC+1 base while being paid against another market's band, usually through a contractor arrangement or an employer-of-record. The remote route is faster and reversible; the relocation route pays more and takes longer. Whichever you pursue, English written communication and a public body of work do more to open the door than any certificate — hiring managers abroad cannot verify your degree easily, but they can read your repositories.

## Frequently asked questions about certifications in Italy

### Do certifications actually get you hired in Italy?

They rarely win an offer on their own, but they do pass filters in cloud, security and data roles, and they help career switchers with no degree signal. Pair every certification with a project that proves you can apply it.

### Which one should I take first?

Match it to the role you want, not to the cheapest option: AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate, Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900) and Google Cloud Associate Cloud Engineer cover the highest-demand paths. Confirm the target role first with the [skill gap analyzer](https://www.skillta.tech/skill-gap-analyzer).

### How reliable are the Italy figures on this page?

They are 2027 market-band estimates built from public compensation datasets and job postings, expressed as annual gross in EUR before tax. Use them as a negotiating anchor, not a guarantee — company type and role scope move a band more than the job title does. Cross-check your own number with the free [salary predictor](https://www.skillta.tech/salary-predictor).

### Do I need to live in Milan and Rome to get hired?

No, but it still helps. Hiring density is highest in Milan, Rome and Turin, and hybrid roles there usually pay above the national band. Fully remote roles have narrowed that gap, and your UTC+1 overlap with the employer's core hours matters more than your postcode for distributed teams.
