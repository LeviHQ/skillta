# The SkillTa Story

Why we built an honest AI career platform — and how your support helps us keep building.

## Where it started

SkillTa started with a simple frustration: thousands of students in India and around the world spend years choosing a tech career based on YouTube hype, random Reddit threads, or whatever their friends picked. There was no honest, personalized guide — just noise, coaching ads, and "top 10 careers" listicles. We wanted to build the tool we wished we had when we were 17: an AI that actually listens, understands your strengths, and gives you a real roadmap — not a sales pitch.

## Our mission

SkillTa exists to make career guidance **honest and high-quality** for students. Our Pro and Lifetime plans include the Career Suite and an ad-free, clean experience. Guests and users without an active plan may see advertisements, helping support the platform.

## The hard truth about running SkillTa

Building SkillTa costs real money every single day. Here's where it goes:

- **AI API bills** — every quiz result you get is generated by an AI model that charges us per request.

- **Domain & hosting** — skillta.tech, server bills, database, and email delivery are ongoing costs.

- **Marketing** — reaching students who need this the most costs money we don't have yet.

- **Development time** — hours of coding, designing, and testing every feature.

## Why we're asking for your help

Plan purchases, guest advertising, and optional donations help cover the ongoing costs of SkillTa. Active Pro and Lifetime users always receive an **ad-free, clean experience**. A donation supports development; it is separate from purchasing a plan.

Every contribution on Ko-fi helps fund AI usage, hosting, and improvements to our career tools. Thank you for supporting an independent project.
