## Who is hiring engineers in India

The India employer landscape splits into four groups, and each has a distinct hiring bar, pay band and interview style. Knowing which group you are applying to is the single most useful piece of preparation you can do.

| Employer type | Examples | Pay | Hiring bar | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Local product leaders | TCS, Infosys and Wipro | High | High | Engineers who want scope and strong peers |
| Global companies hiring locally | Google, Microsoft and Amazon | Highest | Highest | Structured career ladders and mobility |
| Scale-ups and funded startups | Flipkart, Zomato and Razorpay | Medium to high plus equity | Medium | Fast ownership and broad exposure |

## How to get noticed by the companies you actually want

1. Pick eight target companies rather than applying broadly. Depth of research is visible in interviews.
2. Read their engineering blog and job descriptions, then build something adjacent to their problem space.
3. Attend or follow meetups and communities in Bangalore; most referrals start as a conversation, not a request.
4. Message the hiring manager, not only the recruiter. Three sentences: who you are, the closest thing you built, one specific question.
5. Apply within 72 hours of a posting going live. Response rates fall sharply after the first week.

## How to negotiate a tech salary in India

Negotiation in India works best when you anchor on the band rather than on your previous salary. Bring three reference points into the conversation: the published range for the role, what comparable companies in Bangalore and Hyderabad pay for the same scope, and the specific impact you delivered in your last role. India is a fast-growing market where absolute salaries are lower than North America but purchasing power, remote-work arbitrage and career acceleration are strong.

1. Never give the first number. When asked for expectations, respond with the range you researched and ask what band the role is budgeted at.
2. Separate base, bonus, equity and benefits. In India the negotiable levers differ by company size — startups move on equity, enterprises move on base and level.
3. Ask for the level, not just the money. Being hired one level higher compounds across every future raise.
4. Get competing interest. A second live process is the single strongest lever you will ever have.
5. Put the final offer in writing, including start date, notice period, remote policy and review cycle.

> **Reality check:** Most candidates in India leave money on the table by accepting the first offer within 24 hours. Asking for 48 hours to review is standard practice and does not put an offer at risk.

### Which companies pay the most in India?

Global product companies operating locally sit at the top of the range, followed by well-funded local product companies such as TCS and Infosys. Services employers pay least but hire the most juniors.

### Do startups or big companies give better career growth?

Startups give scope earlier; large companies give structure, mentorship and brand value. Early career benefits more from structure, mid-career benefits more from scope.

### How important are referrals?

Very. Referred applications are typically reviewed within days, while cold applications often are not reviewed at all when volume is high.

## Cost of living, tax and what the numbers actually mean

Every figure on this page is annual gross in INR. Take-home pay depends on India's income tax bands, social contributions and any employer-side benefits, so two identical gross offers can differ meaningfully in what reaches your account. Before comparing an offer against another country, convert both to purchasing power rather than exchange rate: a lower headline number in a cheaper city frequently leaves more disposable income than a headline number in Bangalore.

- **Housing** is the largest single variable between Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Gurgaon and Chennai and smaller cities, and it moves effective pay more than a one-level promotion does.
- **Employer type** matters as much as title — funded product companies, global captives and local services firms pay noticeably different bands for the same work.
- **Equity and bonus** are worth discounting heavily unless the company is public or you understand the terms in detail.
- **Remote arbitrage** works both ways: employers increasingly band remote pay by location, so confirm the policy before assuming a hub salary from outside the hub.

## Working internationally from India

Two routes exist and they are very different. Relocation means employer sponsorship, a visa process measured in months, and a compensation reset to the destination market's band. Remote employment means keeping your UTC+5:30 base while being paid against another market's band, usually through a contractor arrangement or an employer-of-record. The remote route is faster and reversible; the relocation route pays more and takes longer. Whichever you pursue, English written communication and a public body of work do more to open the door than any certificate — hiring managers abroad cannot verify your degree easily, but they can read your repositories.

## Frequently asked questions about tech employers in India

### Local employer or global company — which pays better in India?

Global product companies usually pay above the local band and interview harder; local employers such as TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Flipkart, Zomato and Razorpay hire in higher volume and are the more reliable route to a first offer. Many strong careers start at the second and move to the first.

### How do I get a referral?

Engage genuinely before asking — comment on engineering posts, attend meetups in Bangalore, contribute to a project the team uses. A referral from someone who has seen your work converts far better than a cold message asking for one.

### How reliable are the India figures on this page?

They are 2027 market-band estimates built from public compensation datasets and job postings, expressed as annual gross in INR before tax. Use them as a negotiating anchor, not a guarantee — company type and role scope move a band more than the job title does. Cross-check your own number with the free [salary predictor](https://www.skillta.tech/salary-predictor).

### Do I need to live in Bangalore and Hyderabad to get hired?

No, but it still helps. Hiring density is highest in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Gurgaon and Chennai, and hybrid roles there usually pay above the national band. Fully remote roles have narrowed that gap, and your UTC+5:30 overlap with the employer's core hours matters more than your postcode for distributed teams.
